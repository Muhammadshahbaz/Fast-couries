# Competitive Product Blueprint

These notes distill ideas from Run Courier, PostEx, and InstaWorld into the Fast Couriers product direction.

## Competitor Signals

- Run Courier emphasizes simple public tracking, register/login access, fast delivery, safe shipping, and direct contact details.
- PostEx positions itself as ecommerce logistics plus finance: COD, financing, business suite, same-day delivery, track order, merchant signup, and growth capital.
- InstaWorld leans hardest into fintech-enabled logistics: AI courier optimization, same-day payments, support, advance payment, dashboard visibility, integrations, courier partners, insurance, and broad city coverage.

## Fast Couriers Differentiation

- Aggregate couriers instead of operating as a single courier.
- Recommend courier by city, cost, speed, and success rate.
- Make same-day COD settlement a dashboard-first workflow.
- Add returns proof, delivery photo, and dispute evidence from the beginning.
- Offer seller support and account-manager views as product modules, not only phone support.
- Build integrations for WooCommerce, Shopify, and API users as a first-class acquisition path.

## MVP Priorities

1. Seller onboarding with KYC, bank/wallet, and verification status.
2. Courier, city, and rate management.
3. Single booking with recommended courier selection.
4. Seller dashboard with COD, delivery, return, city, and courier quality metrics.
5. Public tracking page with shareable buyer link.
6. Returns and proof-of-delivery timeline.
7. COD payout ledger and same-day settlement batching.

## Later Advantage

- Advance COD financing based on seller delivery history.
- Machine-learning recommendation model after enough delivery outcomes exist.
- Buyer communication automation via WhatsApp and SMS.
- Claims insurance product tied to parcel value and return reasons.
